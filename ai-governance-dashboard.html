How the numbers are calculated

Scope: Individual shows one person's own use; Person + team includes everyone under them. Fake demo data throughout.

In use / Low detected use: an assigned tool counts as in use at 15% or more of the person's allocation for the period (pro-rated by days). Below 15% is Low detected use. It is not the same as waste: it may be a reserved seat, occasional use or a short observation window. Reserved seats are listed separately and excluded from the in-use percentage and from seat utilization; for credits and pay-as-you-go, the dollar allocation still includes them because the credits stay committed.

Detected access: unapproved tools are seen in SSO sign-ins or card expenses. A sign-in shows who accessed a tool, not that they actively used it for work. A card charge shows who paid, not everyone who uses the tool, so employee attribution for card-detected tools is estimated. People counts for unapproved tools mean detected, not confirmed use.

Shadow AI Spend: an estimate, not verified spend. For each employee detected on an unapproved tool, the prototype takes a daily cost from the tool's assumed list price (monthly price ÷ 30), scales it for weekdays vs. weekends and day-to-day variation, and counts only days with detected activity. It approximates potential subscription exposure. It is not taken from card charges or invoices, and it does not measure tokens or credits.

Allocation and status: allocation for a period is the monthly allocation pro-rated by days; the 3-month period counts Sep and Oct in full plus November pro-rated for the days elapsed. Under 40% = Underused (Below budget for pay-as-you-go, which is billed by use), 40–100% = On track, over 100% = Over limit. In a partial month the percentage compares use with the allocation to date, while "left" is measured against the full month; a shared credit pool's "left" is pool-wide. License cost is accrued by days elapsed. Demo assumption: 1 credit = $1 (credit amounts are shown in dollars); actual conversion varies by vendor. "Approved tools outside 40–100%" counts tools across everyone in scope; the team line counts each direct report's team and tool, which is what Recommended actions lists.

Totals: period totals add each month's figure rounded to $1, so they always match the inventory's monthly cells.

Data quality: ✓ Verified = directly from a vendor usage API, invoice or billing record. ≈ Estimated = calculated, inferred or attributed by estimate (all unapproved spend, card-based attribution, and Midjourney, which has no usage API). ? Unknown = not available (billing plan of unapproved tools). Detected access = seen in sign-in logs only.
